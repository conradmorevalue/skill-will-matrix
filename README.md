# Skill/Will Matrix — Grok Skill

A reusable Grok skill that teaches the **Skill/Will Matrix** (Max Landsberg) so leaders stop wasting energy on the wrong people and focus on those who want to grow.

Plot every team member on two axes:

- **Skill** — ability and experience
- **Will** — motivation and drive

This creates four distinct types of people, each requiring a different leadership response:

1. **Top Performers** (High Skill + High Will) — Give ownership, stretch, invest, and build the future around them
2. **High Potentials** (Low Skill + High Will) — Coach, create small wins, pair with mentors
3. **Disengaged Experts** (High Skill + Low Will) — Diagnose the disconnect, reconnect to mission, or exit
4. **Low Performers** (Low Skill + Low Will) — Clear expectations, one chance, then exit respectfully

Inspired by the practical leadership pattern Eric Partaker sees after coaching 650+ CEOs: the strongest teams refuse to manage everyone the same way.

## How to use with Grok

Place the skill folder in your Grok skills directory. It loads automatically on questions about leading teams differently, Skill/Will Matrix, high potentials, underperformers, energy management, people diagnostics, coaching, or focusing on people who want to grow.

Ask things like:
- “Map my team on the Skill/Will Matrix”
- “How should I lead this high-skill but low-will person?”
- “What do I do with a Low Performer who isn’t improving?”
- “Help me stop wasting energy on the wrong people”
- “How do I develop High Potentials into Top Performers?”

**Core ideas**:  
Diagnose before you lead.  
Will often matters more than skill long-term.  
Protect your energy and the team’s standards.  
People change — re-map regularly.
