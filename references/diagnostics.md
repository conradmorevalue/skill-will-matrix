# Diagnostics & Failure Modes for the Skill/Will Matrix

## Common Failure Modes

| Failure Mode | What it looks like | Intervention |
|--------------|--------------------|--------------|
| Uniform management | Same 1:1 style and expectations for everyone | Force a matrix mapping; assign different leadership modes |
| Hoping will improve | Keeping Low Performers or Disengaged Experts indefinitely | Set hard timelines; exit if no movement |
| Under-investing in Top Performers | Assuming high performers “don’t need anything” | Proactively stretch, recognize, and involve them in strategy |
| Starving High Potentials | Giving them work without coaching or feedback | Schedule regular coaching; create small wins and mentor pairings |
| Mislabeling | Calling someone a Top Performer based on past performance | Re-map based on current skill *and* will evidence |
| Ignoring will | Focusing only on skill gaps while motivation is the real issue | Ask diagnostic questions about engagement, purpose, and energy |

## Quick Diagnostic Questions for Any Team Member

- Can this person currently do the job at the required level? (Skill)
- Does this person *want* to do the job and grow in it? (Will)
- What specific evidence supports my placement?
- What leadership response does this quadrant require from me this month?
- Has this person’s skill or will shifted since the last check?

## When to Re-Map

- After a major role change or promotion
- When performance or energy suddenly drops
- Quarterly as a standard leadership practice
- Before deciding on promotions, PIPs, or exits
